Canon is a public forecasting scoreboard: commit a genuine future prediction with a deadline and its source, and a GenLayer round grades it from that source, only after the deadline.

predict(claim, source_url, deadline) commits the claim, a deadline that MUST be in the future, and the source that settles it, bound to the caller and frozen on chain. resolve(id) is refused before the deadline: the contract fetches the source and a round returns CAME_TRUE / DID_NOT / UNCLEAR (RIGHT / WRONG / stays open), recording an evidence fingerprint of the page read.

Source-relative and labelled so: a RIGHT means the committed source showed it came true, not an independent proof. Un-gameable: a past deadline is refused (no backdating), a forecaster cannot register a claim twice (no farming), nobody grades their own. The fetched page is untrusted data; a page that only instructs scores nothing. Proven on chain, injection included.
