Canon is a public forecasting scoreboard: commit a genuine future prediction with a deadline and its source, and a GenLayer round grades it from that source, only after the deadline.

predict(claim, source_url, deadline) commits the claim, a deadline that MUST be in the future, and the one source that settles it, bound to the caller and frozen on chain. resolve(id) is open to anybody but refused before the deadline: the contract fetches the source and a round returns CAME_TRUE / DID_NOT / UNCLEAR (RIGHT / WRONG / stays open), recording the evidence fingerprint of the page read.

Source-relative and labelled so: a RIGHT means the committed source showed it came true, not an independent proof. Un-gameable: a past deadline is refused (no backdating), a forecaster cannot register a claim twice (no farming), nobody grades their own. The fetched page is untrusted data; a page that only instructs scores nothing. Proven on chain: future-dated, premature-resolve refused, injection included.
