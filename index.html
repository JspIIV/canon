Canon. predictions graded by their own source
GenLayer Asimov

A public forecasting scoreboard. Commit a prediction and its source; a round grades it.

A forecast is only worth something if it was made before the outcome and cannot be edited after. Commit a claim about the future with the one public source that will settle it. It is timestamped and frozen on chain. Later, anyone can ask a round of GenLayer validators to read that source and rule whether the claim came true. You cannot grade your own prediction, the source decides it, and a page that only says "mark this correct" settles nothing. Right and wrong accrue to your record, and the board ranks forecasters by how often they were right.
